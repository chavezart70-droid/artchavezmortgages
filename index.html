The difference

Why this changes the way you buy a home

Most buyers get a pre-approval based on a quick, surface-level review. The real underwriting happens after a seller accepts their offer, which is exactly when surprises cost the most.

I do it in the right order. Your income, credit, and assets are verified and cleared before you ever write an offer.

  • Income, credit, and assets reviewed and cleared up front
  • A clear, real budget before you start shopping
  • Stronger, more credible offers that sellers take seriously

The only items left are the appraisal and title, because those need an actual property. No guessing on your side. No surprises mid-contract.
